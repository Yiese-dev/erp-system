\section{Limitations, Future Work and Academic Integrity}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Known Limitations}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item \textbf{Payment gateway.} MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money are
    represented by a disclosed simulator that mimics their request-to-pay and
    signed-callback contract; no real money moves and no production
    credentials are held. Replacing the simulator with a certified adapter is
    future work and is isolated behind the \texttt{PaymentProvider} interface.
  \item \textbf{Statutory payroll rates.} The CNPS/IRPP rule set must be
    reviewed and explicitly approved by an HR officer against the current
    official publications before it is relied upon for anything beyond a
    classroom demonstration; see \cref{sec:hr-module} and
    Appendix~\ref{app:sources}.
  \item \textbf{Biometric attendance.} A signed, time-boxed QR challenge is
    used instead of biometric hardware, as explicitly permitted by the
    project brief.
  \item \textbf{High availability.} A streaming standby is included for the
    backup/recovery story, but automatic failover, multi-region deployment
    and Kubernetes manifests were not attempted (the brief lists Kubernetes as
    optional bonus work).
  \item \textbf{Multi-factor authentication.} Listed as optional bonus work in
    the brief; not implemented in this submission.
  \item \textbf{Deployment scale.} The demonstrated deployment is a single
    Docker host; the scalability strategy in
    \cref{sec:nfr-testing-devops} is validated conceptually (stateless
    services, idempotent consumers) rather than against a multi-node cluster.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Future Work}
Priority candidates for continued development are: a certified mobile-money
adapter; a second, independently verified payroll rule-set review by a
qualified accountant; Kubernetes manifests for the optional bonus marks;
and extending the load-testing workload to the write-heavy payroll-finalisation
and monthly-report-generation paths.
