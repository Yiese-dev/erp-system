\section{Marketing \& Finance Module}
\label{sec:finance-module}

\subsection{Functional Requirements}
\begin{reqlist}
  \setcounter{reqlisti}{10}
  \item A fee plan prices a programme/term combination; changing or
    deactivating a plan never alters invoices already issued, since every
    invoice stores an immutable snapshot of the price it was billed at.
  \item Finance consumes \texttt{academic.enrollment.confirmed.v1} and creates
    exactly one invoice per registration, even under duplicate delivery; a
    registration whose programme/term has no active fee plan becomes a
    visible, retryable billing issue rather than a silent zero-value invoice.
  \item Students initiate an idempotent mobile-money payment (MTN MoMo or
    Orange Money, simulated); the simulator emits a delayed, HMAC-signed
    callback supporting pending, success, failure and timeout outcomes.
  \item Settlement -- payment row, balanced ledger posting, invoice-balance
    update and digital receipt -- commits as one ACID transaction
    (\cref{fig:d3b-sequence-payment-ledger}); a repeated valid callback is a
    safe no-op.
  \item Finance officers can record a manual bank-transfer or cash payment,
    subject to the same outstanding-balance check.
  \item Expenses follow a submitted $\to$ approved $\to$ paid workflow with
    segregation of duties: the approver may never be the submitter.
  \item Marketing campaigns track leads through a fixed pipeline
    (new $\to$ contacted $\to$ qualified $\to$ applied $\to$ converted/lost);
    converting a lead links it to an actually invoiced student, and
    campaign ROI is computed only from tuition genuinely collected after
    conversion, divided by paid campaign spend.
  \item A scheduled worker automatically generates one monthly financial
    summary (collections, receivables, expenses, campaign metrics, all in
    FCFA) per tenant per closed month, catching up after downtime without
    producing duplicates; an authorised user may regenerate a month, which
    creates a new, separately retained version.
  \item The general ledger enforces double-entry invariants at the database
    layer: every posted line is a single positive debit or credit, and every
    entry's debits equal its credits.
\end{reqlist}
