\section{Academic Module}
\label{sec:academic-module}

\subsection{Functional Requirements}
\begin{reqlist}
  \item Administrators can create, update and archive programmes and courses;
    archiving is used instead of deletion whenever a record is referenced by
    historical data, so transcripts remain valid.
  \item Course prerequisites are modelled as a directed graph; adding a
    prerequisite that would create a cycle is rejected.
  \item Registering a student for a term validates that every selected
    course's prerequisites are satisfied by a \emph{published, passing}
    result -- a draft or unpublished grade never satisfies a prerequisite.
  \item A confirmed registration and its enrollments are recorded in one
    transaction together with an outbox event that ultimately triggers
    tuition invoicing (\cref{fig:d3a-sequence-enrollment-invoice}).
  \item Instructors record assessment weights (summing to exactly 100\% before
    publication) and per-student marks; publishing computes the weighted
    final score, letter grade and pass/fail outcome for every enrolled
    student at once.
  \item Attendance is recorded per lesson per enrolled student; a late arrival
    counts as attended, and an excused absence is excluded from the
    computed rate.
  \item Examinations are scheduled with automatic conflict detection across
    room, invigilator and any student enrolled in two overlapping offerings;
    a database-level advisory lock prevents two concurrent bookings from
    both passing the conflict check.
  \item Students may appeal a published result within a configurable window
    (default 14 days); approval creates a new, audited result revision while
    preserving the original mark, never overwriting it.
  \item A documented, transparent rule flags a student as at-risk in a
    course when their attendance falls below a configurable threshold
    (default 75\%) or their latest two published assessment scores are both
    below a configurable failing mark (default 50) -- explicitly a rule, not
    a predictive model.
  \item Students and instructors can export a transcript or attendance
    summary as a branded PDF, generated server-side from currently published
    data only.
\end{reqlist}
